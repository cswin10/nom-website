The old market, reborn

A home for genuinely local food and trade.

Newton Old Market is being brought back to life — a historic market hall in the heart of Newton Abbot, restored as a place where the people who grow it, make it and sell it are at the centre of the story, not the margins.

Market towns like ours have been hollowed out. Local food rarely reaches local tables. The NOM exists to change both — rebuilding the local market as real economic infrastructure: a marketplace connecting South Devon’s producers directly with the people who live here, with radically fewer food miles and far more of every pound staying close to home.

Why it matters

Good food shouldn’t have to travel the world to reach the table next door.

No.1

Local food rarely reaches local tables

Communities face rising food insecurity and reach for lower-cost imported options, while small-scale local producers lack the infrastructure to meet the demands of centralised supermarket procurement.

No.2

Market towns have been hollowed out

Newton Abbot’s town centre has lost over a third of its retail units in a decade. Without a compelling reason to visit, footfall declines and the spaces that once drew people together are eroded or priced out of reach.

∴

The way forward: The NOM

New infrastructure and a living marketplace — connecting local producers directly to local consumers, building an engaged community around flavour and provenance, and anchoring money in the local economy.

Our incentive engine

LEAFI — the Local Economy & Food Incentive.

A simple, radical idea: the more local a trader sources, the less they pay. LEAFI rewards traders for stocking food and goods produced within 50 km of Newton Abbot — from land and sea — tracked automatically at the till and returned as a discount on rent.

  • Encourages sourcing within a 50 km radius of Newton Abbot
  • Tracked and rewarded through unified EPOS integration
  • Discounts on turnover rent that grow as local share grows
  • Designed to shift the whole market local, year on year

The people behind The NOM

Seeds grow here.

The NOM is brought to life by Crop — a community enterprise rebuilding local markets as economic infrastructure, so that South Devon’s food, makers and money can thrive close to home.

The seed has already sprouted: CLASHcrop at the Dartington Cider Press Centre proved the concept — four markets run and a trading community handed on — while Crop’s network of local traders and producers keeps growing, with real trading pathways into the market.
