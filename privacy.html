Privacy policy

Last updated August 2026

The short version: this website does not ask you for anything, and we do not track you on it. There are no forms, no accounts and no analytics.

Who we are

This site is run by Crop, the community enterprise behind Newton Old Market, Market Street, Newton Abbot, Devon TQ12 2RB. For anything to do with this policy or your data, email jay@cropcommunity.org.

To be completed before launch: registered company name and number, registered office if different from the above, and ICO registration number if one is required.

What we collect through this site

Nothing. There are no contact forms, no sign-up boxes and no analytics or advertising trackers. We do not build a profile of you and we do not sell anything to anyone.

If you email us

Every contact link on this site opens your own email program. If you write to us we will hold your message, your email address and anything you choose to tell us, for as long as we need it to reply and to keep a record of the conversation. We use it only to talk to you. We do not add you to a mailing list without asking, and we do not pass it on to anyone else.

Services we load from elsewhere

Two parts of the site are served by Google, which means your device makes a request to Google and Google can see your IP address:

  • Google Fonts, which supplies the typefaces. It does not set cookies.
  • Google Maps, on the Visit section. This one does set cookies, so we do not load it until you ask us to. Until you press the button, no request is made to Google Maps at all.

The site is hosted on Vercel, which keeps standard server logs including IP addresses for security and reliability.

Your rights

Under UK data protection law you can ask us for a copy of any personal data we hold about you, ask us to correct it or delete it, or object to how we are using it. Email us and we will deal with it. If you are not happy with how we have handled it you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk.

Changes

The market is still being built and this site changes often. If we start collecting anything, this page will say so before we do.
